Why Fasih

Fus-ha, not Modern Standard — and not a dialect.

Most Arabic apps teach you to order coffee. Fasih teaches the classical register you actually meet in the texts: fully vocalized, grammatically explained, and reviewed on a schedule that respects how memory works.

Dictionary entries, not flashcards

Every word is a complete record — meanings, plural, masdar, verb forms, roots, synonyms and a usage note — not a bare front and back.

FSRS scheduling, on-device

Reviews are timed by the Free Spaced Repetition Scheduler, running entirely on your phone. Each word returns just before you'd forget it.

Full tashkeel, everywhere

Every headword, plural and example sentence carries complete vocalization — including iʿrāb endings — set in a proper Arabic face.

Private by design

Nothing to sign up for. Nothing to leak.

Fasih's App Store privacy label is "Data Not Collected" — and that's the architecture, not a setting you have to find.

  • No account and no sign-in — the app works the moment it's installed.
  • No ads, no analytics, no tracking, no in-app purchases, no third-party SDKs.
  • Fully offline: the whole dictionary and the scheduler run on your device. Verify it in airplane mode.
  • Delete the app and its data goes with it. Read the full policy.
