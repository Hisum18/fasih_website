Worship & Faith

صَلَاةٌ

ṣalātun · noun · root ص ل و

meanings
prayer, the ritual prayer · blessing (when attributed to God or the angels) · supplication
plural
صَلَوَاتٌ
synonyms
دُعَاءٌ
note
The final ة conceals an original wāw, visible again in the plural.

Three example sentences

أَقَامَ الْمُؤْمِنُ الصَّلَاةَ فِي وَقْتِهَا

The believer performed the prayer at its proper time.

تَجْمَعُ الصَّلَاةُ بَيْنَ الْقِيَامِ وَالرُّكُوعِ وَالسُّجُودِ

The prayer combines standing, bowing, and prostration.

صَلَّى الرَّجُلُ صَلَاةَ الْعِشَاءِ فِي جَمَاعَةٍ

The man prayed the evening prayer in congregation.
